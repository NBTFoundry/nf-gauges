### New

- The engine's values come through NBT Manager's diagnostics, shared with other applications. At install the car says what Gauges may read: the engine, gearbox, transfer case and stability control units, reading only.

### Improved

- TORQUE & POWER: smooth curves without jitter or stray points when the pedal moves or the gearbox shifts; power in horsepower, in white beside the torque.
- TRANSMISSION: a line at the engine's speed on the curves.
- The demo drive follows the dyno pull of an M5.
- The gateway's address is read from the head unit's shared setting first, as other diagnostic tools on the head unit do.

### Good to know

- Needs NBT Manager 0.4.0 or later.
- On a car where NBT Manager reaches no gateway, Gauges shows iDrive's car data but no engine values (seen on a G31). NBT Manager 0.5.5 looks for the gateway by itself.
